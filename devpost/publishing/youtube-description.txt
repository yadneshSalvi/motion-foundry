Want a machine to draw your shape? Motion Foundry helps makers and kinetic artists explore the movement before building it.

Draw a curve, turn the crank, and watch the rods carry the pen. Add a mug stand-in to reveal a collision, then find a new mechanism while keeping the drawing and mounts fixed.

A SPECS Lens demonstrated in Lens Studio Preview.

Try the companion: https://motion-foundry-nine.vercel.app
