Assertions preserve the exact current input, mechanism, candidate identity and verification, then hold the actual QR for eight seconds. Receipts include source clocks and public export fields; signed receipts and credentials are not printed.

The scenario has no fixed r002 hash or parameters. The operator must verify the returned design in the companion before using a handoff shot.
